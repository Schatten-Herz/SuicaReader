# SuicaReader
SuicaReader is a lightweight and intuitive Android application designed for tracking balances and trip histories of Japanese transit IC cards
* **Real-time Card Recognition**: Instantly read the current balance of Suica and other FeliCa-based IC cards via NFC.
* **Comprehensive Trip History**: Automatically parse on-card transaction logs, including subway, train, and bus journeys, as well as top-up (recharge) details.
* **Journey Visualization**: View detailed information for every trip, with routes plotted on a map based on your entry and exit station data.
* **Manual Record Entry**: Support for manually adding trips to fill in any gaps, ensuring your travel ledger remains complete and accurate.
* **Data Import & Export**: Easily back up your trip history to local storage or restore it whenever needed. Your data stays entirely under your control.
